# Time privacy policy

_Last updated November 20, 2026_

Time is an app blocker for iPhone and Mac. It's built to know as little about you as possible.

## What Time collects

Nothing. Time has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. It doesn't send your data to the developer or anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- **The apps, categories, and websites you pick to lock.** On iPhone these come from Apple's Screen Time picker as opaque tokens. Time can't read their names or see which apps you have; iOS only shows them back to you.
- **Your locks:** when each one started and ends, and any emergency unlock in progress.

This is stored only on your device, in Time's own storage. Deleting Time deletes it.

## Screen Time access

On iPhone, Time uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls) only to block the apps and sites you pick, for as long as you choose. It doesn't read your Screen Time usage reports or your activity history.

## Network

The iPhone app makes no network connections. The Mac app contacts public time servers (the drand network, and `apple.com`/`google.com` for their date header) so a lock can't be shortened by changing the clock. None of these requests include anything about you or your apps.

## Children

Time is meant for people limiting their own use. It doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.
